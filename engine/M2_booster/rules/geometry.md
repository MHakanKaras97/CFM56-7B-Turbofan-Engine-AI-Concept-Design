# M2 Geometry Rules (geometry step 1: flow path and blade solids)

Inputs come only from the active record (`records/ACTIVE`, rev001): `results.yaml` (sections, chords, counts, plane
radii and areas) read with `core.records.read_revision`, and `geometry/flowpath.yaml` (6-digit plane radii) used only
after its sha256 matches the record manifest. Station 21 (x, tip radius) is read from
`engine/M1_inlet_fan/geometry/flowpath.yaml`, interface `M2_station_21` (read-only file, not imported).
Rule constants (fractions, ratios, tolerances) are in `geometry/geometry_params.yaml`, each with its tag and note;
this file states the rule, the yaml holds the value. One tag per rule.

| Id | Rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| R-G2.1 | **Frame** = M1 convention (`engine/M1_inlet_fan/geometry/frame.py`, read, not imported): X engine axis positive aft, origin on the axis in the fan rotor stacking plane, Y/Z radial plane, metres; reference blade at +Z (radial), tangential +Y; rotation positive about +X, so at +Z the blade moves towards -Y. Blade k at k x 360/N about +X. | [T] |
| R-G2.2 | **Axial layout**: rows R1 S1 R2 S2 R3 S3 in this order. Row axial width b = largest c cos(gamma) of its hub/mean/tip sections (c record chord, gamma stagger of R-G2.3). Inlet gap from station 21 to R1 LE and every gap after a row = `axial_gap_fraction` x b of the upstream row (station 21 for the inlet gap: R1 width). Station 24 = S3 TE plane; transition duct 24 -> 25 not modelled (station 25 = station 24 plane, placeholder). | [T] |
| R-G2.3 | **Section**: planar section at the section radius r = (r_le + r_te)/2 of the record row (hub, mean, tip). Metal angles = record flow angles (zero incidence, zero deviation). Circular-arc camber line, camber theta = k1 - k2, stagger gamma = (k1 + k2)/2. Thickness laid off normal to the camber line: t(u) = t_e + (t_max - t_e)(1 - (2u/c - 1)^2) (parabolic stand-in for the double-circular-arc family of TP-1523), blunt LE/TE of thickness t_e. t/c and t_e/c from `geometry_params.yaml`. Stacking: chord midpoints on a radial line at the row's mid-axial plane, tangential 0. | [T] |
| R-G2.4 | **Span and trim**: the loft runs through hub, mean and tip sections plus one copy of the hub and tip sections moved outward by an extension, then is cut by the revolved flow path. Rotor: from the hub line to (casing - tip clearance). Stator: hub line to casing (no clearance, attachment not chosen). | [T] |
| R-G2.5 | **Flow path**: casing radius constant at the station 21 tip radius; hub line through the record plane radii (row LE and TE planes, piecewise linear across a row, constant in the gaps, where the TE of one row and the LE of the next share the record plane). | [T] |
| R-G2.6 | **Tip clearance** (rotors) = `tip_clearance_ratio` x mean rotor blade height (mean of LE and TE annulus heights); ratio = TP-1523 rotor 23B average static clearance / rig blade height, proportional transfer (no CFM56 value). | [T] |
| R-G2.7 | **Material for mass**: rotor blades `ti6al4v` (materials.md); stator vanes `tbd` (materials.md), mass uses the `ti6al4v` density as a placeholder; density read from `tools/data/materials.yaml`. Mass is informational, not an acceptance value. Open issue #71 (draft). | [T] |
| R-G2.8 | **Checks**: annulus area of a planar cut of the revolved flow path at every record plane vs the record area, relative tolerance `area_tolerance`; mean-section chord and solidity (aero_method.md definition, r_mean = mean of LE/TE radii) measured on the solid vs the record, relative tolerance `chord_solidity_tolerance`; root and tip vertex radii on the hub line and on (casing - clearance) within `radius_tolerance_m`; neighbouring blades of one row do not intersect (common volume 0, distance > 0). No constant is tuned to pass a check. | [T] |

Stop rule: a section that cannot be built within these rules gets a [T] placeholder and an open issue draft in
`open_items.md`; the geometry continues.

Note for M0 (shared frame): M1 `frame.py` mixes the frame definition with M1 helpers (record access, STEP cache).
Recommendation: a small `core/frame.py` with the frame constants and axis-only helpers (rotation about the axis,
revolve of a meridional curve, radius of a point, STEP export to `.cache/geometry/<module>/<rev>/`), imported by M1
and M2+; module-specific record access stays in each module. Until then M2 re-implements the convention locally
(R-G2.1) and a test checks the M1 and M2 constants agree. Creation needs a CR (core/ and M1 are not M2's).

## Geometry step 2: rotor drum, inner shrouds, casing (R-G2.9-17)

Written before the step 2 code (`geometry/hardware.py`). Inputs as in step 1 (active record rev001, step 1 layout and
blades); hardware sizes are ratios in `geometry_params.yaml` (keys `hw_*`, one tag per field) until the structural
stage (A-4); none is tuned to pass a check.

| Id | Rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| R-G2.9 | **Rotating / stationary split**: every inter-row gap (R-G2.2) is split at its axial mid-plane; an axial running gap of `hw_running_gap_over_row_gap` x gap is centred on it. Rotor rims extend to the running gaps; stator inner shrouds fill the rest. Rim 1 starts at the station 21 plane (start of the M2 hub flow-path line; the inlet gap belongs to the rotating hub); the S3 shroud and the drum end at the station 24 plane. Station 21 is the flow plane only, not a hardware boundary: the M2 hardware boundary is the M1 fan disk aft face (x read at run time from the M1 rev014 geometry code, R-G2.13); between that face and station 21 the drum carries the forward cone (R-G2.18). This removes "nothing ahead of station 21" as a hardware rule (step 3a, #76). | [T] |
| R-G2.10 | **Rotor drum (M2.0.1, one solid)**: one rim per rotor row; rim outer surface = hub flow path line (R-G2.5) over the rim length. Blade attachment = simplified straight slot, one per blade, along the hub-section stagger (R-G2.3) in the tangent plane, rectangular section: width `hw_slot_width_over_normal_pitch` x hub pitch x cos(stagger), centred on the hub-section camber line at half the camber sag, bottom radius = smallest rim hub radius - `hw_slot_depth_over_rotor_chord` x rotor chord (step 3a: superseded by the sloped bottom of R-G2.19; this radius stays the live-rim reference), open at both rim faces. Live rim below the slot: depth `hw_rim_depth_over_slot_depth` x slot depth. Under each stator the rims are joined by a cylindrical drum wall (outer radius = seal tooth root, R-G2.11; thickness `hw_drum_wall_over_hub_radius` x stator hub radius); a rim reaches down at least to the inner radius of the adjacent walls. Blade root block (part of M2.n.1, not fused) = slot section shrunk by `hw_fit_clearance_over_slot_width` x slot width on the sides and bottom, top = hub line. No disc webs, bores, shims or spacers. | [T] |
| R-G2.11 | **Stator inner shroud (proposed M2.n.4, full ring) with seal land; drum seal teeth**: shroud outer surface = hub flow path line over the shroud length (R-G2.9); thickness `hw_shroud_thickness_over_stator_chord` x stator chord below the smallest hub radius of the row; seal land ring under it, thickness `hw_land_thickness_over_stator_chord` x stator chord over `hw_land_length_over_shroud_length` x shroud length, centred. The drum wall carries `hw_tooth_count` rectangular seal teeth under each land (width `hw_tooth_width_over_land_length` x land length, height `hw_tooth_height_over_land_thickness` x land thickness, equal spacing), tip radius = land inner radius - radial seal clearance; clearance = R-G2.6 tip clearance of the upstream rotor x `hw_seal_clearance_over_tip_clearance`. Vanes end on the shroud outer surface (no vane slot in the shroud). | [T] |
| R-G2.12 | **Casing (M2.0.2)**: revolved shell from station 21 to station 24, inner surface = casing radius (R-G2.5) = tip flow path; rotor tip clearance stays the R-G2.6 rule, measured to the casing inner surface (abradable liners M2.n.3 not modelled, no recess). Thickness `hw_casing_thickness_over_radius` x casing radius. One circumferential vane slot (groove) per stator row: axial extent = row width + 2 x `hw_vane_slot_margin_over_row_width` x row width, depth `hw_vane_slot_depth_over_stator_chord` x stator chord. Vane foot (part of M2.n.2, not fused) = annular sector in the groove, span `hw_foot_span_over_pitch` x 360/N, shrunk by the fit clearance (R-G2.10 ratio x groove depth) axially and radially; inner face on the casing radius. Ribs, flanges, bolts and the VBV ports (M1) not modelled. | [T] |
| R-G2.13 | **Drum forward end and M1 interface**: the stage-1 drum flange = annular plate on the station 21 plane (thickness `hw_flange_thickness_over_drum_wall` x drum wall of stage 1), from the M1 disk live-rim inner radius up to rim 1; the forward cone (R-G2.18) joins it ahead of station 21. The M1 disk aft-face x, live-rim inner radius, slot-bottom radius and the bolt circle (info: mean of the live-rim inner and slot-bottom radius) are read at run time from the M1 rev014 geometry code (`engine/M1_inlet_fan/geometry/rotor_hardware.py` `build`, read-only import, no M1 file written); they are never typed (the former `m1_disk_*` copies in `geometry_params.yaml` are removed). The hardware boundary is the M1 disk aft face, not the station 21 plane ("no M2 part crosses station 21" no longer applies). Bolt holes in the M1 disk are an M1 feature and are not modelled (interface note, #76). | [T] |
| R-G2.14 | **Drum aft end**: the drum ends at the station 24 plane with the S3 shroud seal teeth; no aft disc, no shaft connection (the booster is driven through the fan disk, J2-1; LP shaft and bearings are M8). | [T] |
| R-G2.15 | **Drum ownership**: the booster spool (drum) is M2's part M2.0.1, confirmed by the user (bom.md plan; architecture.md section 4 gives M8 shafts, bearing supports and sumps, not compressor rotors). The booster spool LLP life (22,900 vs 23,600 EFC conflict still open, #49) is in M2 `known_values.md`; M8 keeps a one-line pointer. | [T] |
| R-G2.16 | **Hardware material for mass**: drum and root blocks `ti6al4v` (materials.md); casing, shrouds, vane feet `tbd`, mass with the `ti6al4v` density as placeholder (#71). Mass is information only. | [T] |
| R-G2.17 | **Step 2 checks**: drum maximum radius at planar cuts inside each rim = hub line; shroud maximum radius = hub line; casing minimum radius = casing radius; rotor tip-to-casing distance = R-G2.6 clearance; common volume (relative to the smaller solid, `hw_common_volume_tolerance`) 0 between every pair of M2 parts (blade airfoils, root blocks, slotted drum, shrouds, casing, vane feet; neighbouring root blocks and feet); rotating vs stationary parts at distance > 0; root-section footprint of each airfoil inside its root block top and vane tip footprint inside its foot (section points, R-G2.3); no part with x below the M1 disk aft face (hardware boundary, R-G2.9/R-G2.13; the station 21 plane is not checked as a hardware limit); wall thicknesses (live rim, drum wall, casing behind the groove) >= `hw_min_wall_m`. M1 parts are not M2 neighbours: overlap with M1 parts is reported, not fixed. | [T] |

## Geometry step 3a: drum forward cone (#76) and sloped slot bottom (#75) (R-G2.18-19)

Written before the step 3a code (`geometry/hardware.py`). No new constant: both rules reuse step 2 ratios. The M1 values
(disk aft face, live-rim inner and slot-bottom radii, M8 bearing-flange inner radius) are read at run time from the
M1 rev014 geometry code (read-only import).
The casing (M2.0.2) and the #77 items are not changed here (they wait for CR-002/CR-003).

| Id | Rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| R-G2.18 | **Drum forward cone (part of M2.0.1, one solid with the drum)**: flange ring on the M1 disk aft face, axial thickness = R-G2.13 flange thickness (`hw_flange_thickness_over_drum_wall` x stage-1 drum wall), radially from the M1 disk live-rim inner radius to the M1 disk slot-bottom radius; conical shell of the stage-1 drum wall thickness (R-G2.10 rule `hw_drum_wall_over_hub_radius`, the same wall as under S1), centred on the bolt circle at the aft face of the ring, running down to the inner edge of the stage-1 drum flange at station 21 (shell inner radius there = flange inner radius). Bolt holes and bolts not modelled (M1 precedent: spinner flange bolted to the disk front face, bolts and holes not modelled, M1 `geometry/spinner_case.py`, `case_params.yaml`). Radial clearance from the smallest drum radius to the M8 bearing-flange boundary (M1 `fan_frame.interfaces` `M8_bearing_support` `r_inner_m`, read-only) must stay > 0. Mass counted once, inside M2.0.1 (the M1 BOM has no aft disk arm); the cone mass is reported as information (R-G2.16 density). Checks: common volume 0 (R-G2.17 tolerance) between the cone and every M1 rotor part (fan disk, fan blade with root, shim, spacer, platform, spinner; instance 0 is exact because the cone is axisymmetric) and between the whole drum and those parts; cone-to-disk distance 0 (contact on the aft face); M8 clearance > 0; no M2 part ahead of the disk aft face. If the cone cannot be built within this rule: stop rule (placeholder, open issue). | [T] |
| R-G2.19 | **Sloped rotor slot bottom (#75, option 1)**: the slot bottom is parallel to the local hub line: bottom radius at x = hub line r(x) - `hw_slot_depth_over_rotor_chord` x rotor chord over the rim length (piecewise linear like the hub line, R-G2.5); ratio 0.25 unchanged, slot width, direction and section of R-G2.10 unchanged. The smallest slot-bottom radius (rim front face) stays the live-rim reference, so live rim and rim inner radius are as in R-G2.10. Root block = slot section shrunk by the fit clearance on the sides and radially at the bottom (bottom = slot bottom + fit clearance), top = hub line. Rationale (general engineering knowledge, [G] in the note): on a conical rim an axial dovetail follows the rim cone, so the neck/lobe section and the load-bearing depth of the root are the same along the root; a constant-radius bottom under a rising hub would make the root deeper aft without carrying more load. The rule is chosen for this reason, not to make the R-G2.10 "open at both rim faces" condition pass. Record per stage: aft-face margin = slot bottom at the rim aft face - drum wall outer radius of the next stator (R-G2.11) and front-face margin = slot bottom at the rim front face - drum wall outer radius of the previous stator; both are checks for the structural stage (A-4 root/rim sizing; J2-2 dovetail type doğrulanamadı), not a pass. Checks: aft-face margin > 0 at all three stages, root block common volume 0 with the slotted drum, root block-to-drum distance > 0 and <= fit clearance. | [T] |

## Geometry step 3b: hub flow-path wall (#79) (R-G2.20)

Written before the step 3b code (`geometry/hardware.py` `hub_wall`, `hub_wall_checks`). One new constant
(`hw_wall_joint_gap_over_wall` in `geometry_params.yaml`). Owner decision (user, #79): the wall is M2's, part of M2.0.1.
No BOM change, no revision, no M1 file changed.

| Id | Rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| R-G2.20 | **Hub flow-path wall (part of M2.0.1, one solid with the drum and the forward cone)**: forward extension of the drum outer skin; cylindrical shell from the M1 platform aft end + axial joint gap to the station 21 plane. M1 platform aft end x = largest x of the M1 rev014 platform solid, read at run time via `m1_interface` (read-only import, never typed). Outer radius = hub flow-path radius at station 21 of the record (M2 plane 0 = M1 `flowpath.yaml` interface `M2_station_21` `r_hub_m`, layout check); the M1 hub line (`curves_x_r_m.hub`) must have the same radius at the platform aft end and at station 21, else stop rule. Thickness = stage-1 drum wall thickness rule (R-G2.10 `hw_drum_wall_over_hub_radius` x smallest S1 hub radius, the same wall as under S1 and the cone shell of R-G2.18). Joined to the drum on the rim 1 front face at station 21 (wall inner radius above the rim 1 inner radius); the forward cone (R-G2.18) meets the same station 21 flange from below, so wall, flange and cone form one solid. Axial joint gap to the M1 platform = `hw_wall_joint_gap_over_wall` (0.1) x hub wall thickness: assembly gap between co-rotating faces of the same spool, no running clearance; no source (doğrulanamadı). Mass counted once, in M2.0.1 (wall mass reported as information, R-G2.16 density). Checks: wall outer radius on the drum solid = hub flow-path radius within `radius_tolerance_m`; common volume 0 (R-G2.17 tolerance) of the wall region with every M1 rotor part (fan disk, blade with root, shim, spacer, platform, spinner), the forward cone, the M2 airfoils/vanes and root blocks (axisymmetric wall, instance 0 exact); hub line from the M1 blade hub TE to station 21: no uncovered axial interval larger than the joint gap; drum covers the hub line from its start to station 21 without a gap; the radial band of the rim 1 slot front opening covered by the wall is recorded (R-G2.10/19 "open at both rim faces": any cover is a stop-rule entry). If the wall cannot be built or a check fails: stop rule (no tuning, open issue). | [T] |

Values from code (`hardware.py` `hub_wall_checks`, rev001, M1 rev014): joint gap 0.000485 m [H] (0.1 x 4.852 mm wall);
wall x 0.140777-0.235369 m, r 0.297278-0.30213 m [H]. The literal hub-line check fails because of the M1 platform
shape (open issue #80 draft in `open_items.md`), not because of the wall. The wall covers the upper 4.852 mm of the
R1 slot front opening (window 10.236 mm below it) and touches the R1 root blocks (distance 0, R-G2.19 needs > 0):
stop rule, wall kept as the rule gives it (placeholder), open issue #81 draft.
