# M2 Geometry Rules (geometry step 1: flow path and blade solids)

Inputs come only from the active record (`records/ACTIVE`, rev001): `results.yaml` (sections, chords, counts, plane
radii and areas) read with `core.records.read_revision`, and `geometry/flowpath.yaml` (6-digit plane radii) used only
after its sha256 matches the record manifest. Station 21 (x, tip radius) is read from
`engine/M1_inlet_fan/geometry/flowpath.yaml`, interface `M2_station_21` (read-only file, not imported).
Rule constants (fractions, ratios, tolerances) are in `geometry/geometry_params.yaml`, each with its tag and note;
this file states the rule, the yaml holds the value. One tag per rule.

| Id | Rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| R-G2.1 | **Frame** = M1 convention (`engine/M1_inlet_fan/geometry/frame.py`, read, not imported): X engine axis positive aft, origin on the axis in the fan rotor stacking plane, Y/Z radial plane, metres; reference blade at +Z (radial), tangential +Y; rotation positive about +X, so at +Z the blade moves towards -Y. Blade k at k x 360/N about +X. | [T] |
| R-G2.2 | **Axial layout**: rows R1 S1 R2 S2 R3 S3 in this order. Row axial width b = largest c cos(gamma) of its hub/mean/tip sections (c record chord, gamma stagger of R-G2.3). Inlet gap from station 21 to R1 LE and every gap after a row = `axial_gap_fraction` x b of the upstream row (station 21 for the inlet gap: R1 width). Station 24 = S3 TE plane; transition duct 24 -> 25 not modelled (station 25 = station 24 plane, placeholder). | [T] |
| R-G2.3 | **Section**: planar section at the section radius r = (r_le + r_te)/2 of the record row (hub, mean, tip). Metal angles = record flow angles (zero incidence, zero deviation). Circular-arc camber line, camber theta = k1 - k2, stagger gamma = (k1 + k2)/2. Thickness laid off normal to the camber line: t(u) = t_e + (t_max - t_e)(1 - (2u/c - 1)^2) (parabolic stand-in for the double-circular-arc family of TP-1523), blunt LE/TE of thickness t_e. t/c and t_e/c from `geometry_params.yaml`. Stacking: chord midpoints on a radial line at the row's mid-axial plane, tangential 0. | [T] |
| R-G2.4 | **Span and trim**: the loft runs through hub, mean and tip sections plus one copy of the hub and tip sections moved outward by an extension, then is cut by the revolved flow path. Rotor: from the hub line to (casing - tip clearance). Stator: hub line to casing (no clearance, attachment not chosen). | [T] |
| R-G2.5 | **Flow path**: casing radius constant at the station 21 tip radius; hub line through the record plane radii (row LE and TE planes, piecewise linear across a row, constant in the gaps, where the TE of one row and the LE of the next share the record plane). | [T] |
| R-G2.6 | **Tip clearance** (rotors) = `tip_clearance_ratio` x mean rotor blade height (mean of LE and TE annulus heights); ratio = TP-1523 rotor 23B average static clearance / rig blade height, proportional transfer (no CFM56 value). | [T] |
| R-G2.7 | **Material for mass**: rotor blades `ti6al4v` (materials.md); stator vanes `tbd` (materials.md), mass uses the `ti6al4v` density as a placeholder; density read from `tools/data/materials.yaml`. Mass is informational, not an acceptance value. Open issue #71 (draft). | [T] |
| R-G2.8 | **Checks**: annulus area of a planar cut of the revolved flow path at every record plane vs the record area, relative tolerance `area_tolerance`; mean-section chord and solidity (aero_method.md definition, r_mean = mean of LE/TE radii) measured on the solid vs the record, relative tolerance `chord_solidity_tolerance`; root and tip vertex radii on the hub line and on (casing - clearance) within `radius_tolerance_m`; neighbouring blades of one row do not intersect (common volume 0, distance > 0). No constant is tuned to pass a check. | [T] |

Stop rule: a section that cannot be built within these rules gets a [T] placeholder and an open issue draft in
`open_items.md`; the geometry continues.

Note for M0 (shared frame): M1 `frame.py` mixes the frame definition with M1 helpers (record access, STEP cache).
Recommendation: a small `core/frame.py` with the frame constants and axis-only helpers (rotation about the axis,
revolve of a meridional curve, radius of a point, STEP export to `.cache/geometry/<module>/<rev>/`), imported by M1
and M2+; module-specific record access stays in each module. Until then M2 re-implements the convention locally
(R-G2.1) and a test checks the M1 and M2 constants agree. Creation needs a CR (core/ and M1 are not M2's).

## Geometry step 2: rotor drum, inner shrouds, casing (R-G2.9-17)

Written before the step 2 code (`geometry/hardware.py`). Inputs as in step 1 (active record rev001, step 1 layout and
blades); hardware sizes are ratios in `geometry_params.yaml` (keys `hw_*`, one tag per field) until the structural
stage (A-4); none is tuned to pass a check. Ids are written in bold so that the step 1 table above keeps its 8 rows.

| Id | Rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **R-G2.9** | **Rotating / stationary split**: every inter-row gap (R-G2.2) is split at its axial mid-plane; an axial running gap of `hw_running_gap_over_row_gap` x gap is centred on it. Rotor rims extend to the running gaps; stator inner shrouds fill the rest. Rim 1 starts at the station 21 plane (the inlet gap belongs to the rotating hub; upstream of station 21 is M1); the S3 shroud and the drum end at the station 24 plane. | [T] |
| **R-G2.10** | **Rotor drum (M2.0.1, one solid)**: one rim per rotor row; rim outer surface = hub flow path line (R-G2.5) over the rim length. Blade attachment = simplified straight slot, one per blade, along the hub-section stagger (R-G2.3) in the tangent plane, rectangular section: width `hw_slot_width_over_normal_pitch` x hub pitch x cos(stagger), centred on the hub-section camber line at half the camber sag, bottom radius = smallest rim hub radius - `hw_slot_depth_over_rotor_chord` x rotor chord, open at both rim faces. Live rim below the slot: depth `hw_rim_depth_over_slot_depth` x slot depth. Under each stator the rims are joined by a cylindrical drum wall (outer radius = seal tooth root, R-G2.11; thickness `hw_drum_wall_over_hub_radius` x stator hub radius); a rim reaches down at least to the inner radius of the adjacent walls. Blade root block (part of M2.n.1, not fused) = slot section shrunk by `hw_fit_clearance_over_slot_width` x slot width on the sides and bottom, top = hub line. No disc webs, bores, shims or spacers. | [T] |
| **R-G2.11** | **Stator inner shroud (proposed M2.n.4, full ring) with seal land; drum seal teeth**: shroud outer surface = hub flow path line over the shroud length (R-G2.9); thickness `hw_shroud_thickness_over_stator_chord` x stator chord below the smallest hub radius of the row; seal land ring under it, thickness `hw_land_thickness_over_stator_chord` x stator chord over `hw_land_length_over_shroud_length` x shroud length, centred. The drum wall carries `hw_tooth_count` rectangular seal teeth under each land (width `hw_tooth_width_over_land_length` x land length, height `hw_tooth_height_over_land_thickness` x land thickness, equal spacing), tip radius = land inner radius - radial seal clearance; clearance = R-G2.6 tip clearance of the upstream rotor x `hw_seal_clearance_over_tip_clearance`. Vanes end on the shroud outer surface (no vane slot in the shroud). | [T] |
| **R-G2.12** | **Casing (M2.0.2)**: revolved shell from station 21 to station 24, inner surface = casing radius (R-G2.5) = tip flow path; rotor tip clearance stays the R-G2.6 rule, measured to the casing inner surface (abradable liners M2.n.3 not modelled, no recess). Thickness `hw_casing_thickness_over_radius` x casing radius. One circumferential vane slot (groove) per stator row: axial extent = row width + 2 x `hw_vane_slot_margin_over_row_width` x row width, depth `hw_vane_slot_depth_over_stator_chord` x stator chord. Vane foot (part of M2.n.2, not fused) = annular sector in the groove, span `hw_foot_span_over_pitch` x 360/N, shrunk by the fit clearance (R-G2.10 ratio x groove depth) axially and radially; inner face on the casing radius. Ribs, flanges, bolts and the VBV ports (M1) not modelled. | [T] |
| **R-G2.13** | **Drum forward end and M1 interface**: forward flange of the drum = annular plate on the station 21 plane (thickness `hw_flange_thickness_over_drum_wall` x drum wall of stage 1), from the M1 disk live-rim inner radius up to rim 1; flange bolt circle (info) = mean of the M1 disk live-rim inner radius and slot-bottom radius. M1 disk values are read-only (M1 `geometry/rotor_hardware.py` build, rev014) and copied into `geometry_params.yaml` as `m1_disk_*` [H] with a test that recomputes them. No M2 part crosses the station 21 plane; the axial gap from the M1 disk aft face to the flange is an interface note for M0 (no CR, M1 not edited). | [T] |
| **R-G2.14** | **Drum aft end**: the drum ends at the station 24 plane with the S3 shroud seal teeth; no aft disc, no shaft connection (the booster is driven through the fan disk, J2-1; LP shaft and bearings are M8). | [T] |
| **R-G2.15** | **Drum ownership**: the booster spool (drum) is assumed M2's part M2.0.1 (bom.md plan; architecture.md section 4 gives M8 shafts, bearing supports and sumps, not compressor rotors). M8 `known_values.md` lists the booster spool LLP life (22,900 vs 23,600 EFC conflict, open) as a source value only. Not decided here: assumption for M0/user. | [T] |
| **R-G2.16** | **Hardware material for mass**: drum and root blocks `ti6al4v` (materials.md); casing, shrouds, vane feet `tbd`, mass with the `ti6al4v` density as placeholder (#71). Mass is information only. | [T] |
| **R-G2.17** | **Step 2 checks**: drum maximum radius at planar cuts inside each rim = hub line; shroud maximum radius = hub line; casing minimum radius = casing radius; rotor tip-to-casing distance = R-G2.6 clearance; common volume (relative to the smaller solid, `hw_common_volume_tolerance`) 0 between every pair of M2 parts (blade airfoils, root blocks, slotted drum, shrouds, casing, vane feet; neighbouring root blocks and feet); rotating vs stationary parts at distance > 0; root-section footprint of each airfoil inside its root block top and vane tip footprint inside its foot (section points, R-G2.3); no part with x below the station 21 plane; wall thicknesses (live rim, drum wall, casing behind the groove) >= `hw_min_wall_m`. M1 parts are not M2 neighbours: overlap with M1 parts is reported, not fixed. | [T] |
