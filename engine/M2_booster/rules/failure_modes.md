# M2 Failure Modes

| Id | Mode | Why it matters for the booster | Concept-level handling | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FM2-1 | Foreign object, ice and hail ingestion | Material that passes the fan hub enters the booster; ice shed from the spinner/fan or intake | Rule only: no analysis at step 1; CS-E 540 (strike and ingestion, PDF s. 104-105), CS-E 780(f) ice ingestion (s. 147-148), CS-E 790 hail (s. 162-163) set the test conditions [K] | EASA CS-E Amdt 5 (M1 archive, sources #39) |
| FM2-2 | Stall / surge | Booster works between fan and HPC; at low power the HPC swallows less than the booster delivers | VBV (flow function M2, hardware M1 frame); not modelled at the design point; DF limits R-AM2.2 keep design-point loading in the tested range | Girão 2023 PDF s. 36-37, 47 [KS]; RM E53D01 [K] |
| FM2-3 | Rotor tip rub | Small clearances on a short-span blade | Abradable liner (M2.n.3); clearance [T] | TP-1523 Table 18 [K] as scale |
| FM2-4 | Drum low-cycle fatigue | The booster spool is a life-limited part | Hoop stress margin (A-4); LLP 22,900 EFC [KS] as context, no life calculation | Owner's Guide mtce s. 19-20 [KS] |
| FM2-5 | Blade high-cycle fatigue | Low-aspect-ratio vs high-aspect-ratio blades; wakes of the fan and stators | Campbell check later (not at step 1) | doğrulanamadı (no source in the M2 set) |
