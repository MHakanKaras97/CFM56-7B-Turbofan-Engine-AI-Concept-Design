# M1 Aerodynamic Method (meanline)

Summary of the meanline theses in `learning/sources/aero/`. Page numbers are PDF pages. No code yet; this file fixes the method the M1 meanline code will follow.

## Method steps (meanline, per blade row)
1. Inputs: mass flow, inlet Tt and Pt, rotor speed, annulus radii at inlet and exit, blade angles, solidity, thickness/chord, blockage. Perfect gas (Visentin p. 27) [KS]; M1 uses the M0 gas model instead [G].
2. Axial velocity from continuity with a blockage factor; relative Mach found by Newton iteration on the static state (Visentin pp. 29-30) [KS].
3. Inlet velocity triangle; incidence = inlet relative flow angle minus blade inlet angle (Abrisha p. 36) [KS].
4. Deviation: reference value at minimum-loss incidence from a Carter-type rule (camber, solidity, thickness, stagger), then corrected for off-reference incidence (Sjögren pp. 22-23; Abrisha p. 36) [KS].
5. Exit triangle and work from the Euler equation, delta h = U2 Vt2 - U1 Vt1; rothalpy conserved across the rotor (Visentin p. 30; Abrisha p. 36) [KS].
6. Losses as total pressure loss coefficients, summed: profile + shock + endwall (+ tip clearance, secondary) (Sjögren p. 22; Visentin p. 31) [KS].
7. Exit total pressure from the loss; efficiency and pressure ratio from the total temperature and pressure ratios (Abrisha pp. 36-37) [KS].
8. Off-design: incidence-dependent loss bucket; stall and choke limits from critical incidence (Abrisha pp. 41-42); SATURN flags choke when the Mach loop diverges and surge from a stall criterion (Visentin p. 32) [KS].
9. Spanwise: SATURN solves several spans per row (Visentin p. 27); SC90C adds spanwise mixing and an endwall loss distribution along the span (Sjögren p. 23) [KS].

## Loss models
### Diffusion factor (blade loading)
- Lieblein diffusion factor DF = 1 - W2/W1 + |delta Vt| / (2 sigma W1) (Sjögren eq. 2.13; Abrisha eq. 4.10) [KS].
- Equivalent diffusion ratio D_eq (Lieblein) gives the wake momentum thickness and hence profile loss (Sjögren eq. 2.10-2.11) [KS].
- Abrisha caps DF at 0.7 in the Koch-Smith model (p. 38) and gives 0.3-0.6 as a practical design criterion, 0.3 at the tip and 0.6 at the hub (p. 38, printed p. 26) [KS].

### Profile loss
- Lieblein: loss from wake momentum thickness vs D_eq and inlet Mach (Sjögren p. 23) [KS].
- Koch and Smith: includes compressibility, area contraction, thickness; chosen by Abrisha as the conservative option (pp. 37-38) [KS].
- Hearsey (exponential in DF) and Miller (Mach-interpolated polynomials) as alternatives (Abrisha p. 39) [KS].
- Reynolds correction below Re = 1e6 (Sjögren eq. 2.16, p. 25) [KS].

### Shock loss
- Normal-shock total pressure loss at a passage Mach number = mean of inlet relative Mach and peak suction-surface Mach (Schwenk method in SC90C, Sjögren p. 24) [KS].
- Taper between M1rel = 0.8 (zero) and 1.0 (full) (Sjögren p. 24) [KS].
- Miller-Lewis-Hartmann / Koch-Smith in SATURN (Visentin p. 32; Zanoni p. 20) [KS].
- Boyer's modified Miller-Bloch: oblique shock with sonic downstream flow, then a second normal shock if still supersonic (Abrisha p. 40) [KS].

### Endwall, tip clearance, secondary
- Endwall: Koch-Smith, Koch, Benner (SATURN); Wright-Miller with aspect ratio, loading and clearance (SC90C); polynomial in DF with a cap (Abrisha p. 40) [KS].
- Tip clearance: Yaras-Sjolander; secondary: Howell (Visentin p. 32) [KS].

## Implementation (M1 step 2)
- `tools/meanline.py` (shared with M2/M3): stream tubes with given inlet/exit radii and areas, no radial equilibrium; perfect gas with gamma 1.4 [G].
- Deviation: Carter's rule (m = 0.23 (2a/c)^2 + kappa2/500, a/c = 0.5) [G], at the reference incidence only.
- Profile loss: Lieblein D_eq [KS] with the wake momentum fit theta/c = 0.004 (1 + 3.1 (D_eq - 1)^2 + 0.4 (D_eq - 1)^8) [G, Aungier 2003, not in the source set].
- Shock loss: normal shock at the mean of inlet relative Mach and the suction-surface Mach after a Prandtl-Meyer expansion to the covered-passage entrance [KS method, geometry assumption T].
- Endwall and tip clearance loss (M1 step 3b, `Endwall` data per tube; off when absent). No source in the set gives a closed form with coefficients (Abrisha p. 40 gives the structure but not the polynomial coefficients; Sjögren eq. 2.12 leaves the Wright-Miller function f(D, tau/c) to its reference), so general correlations are used:
  - endwall: Howell annulus drag C_Da = 0.020 s/h + secondary drag C_Ds = 0.018 C_L^2, loss = sigma C_D cos^2(beta1) / cos^3(beta_m) [G; Howell is SATURN's secondary model, Visentin p. 32];
  - tip clearance: Lakshminarayana efficiency debit 0.7 (tau/h) psi / cos(beta_m) (1 + 10 sqrt((tau/c) AR / (psi cos(beta_m)))) [G], applied as the matching total pressure loss;
  - spanwise weights: endwall 3 (2f - 1)^2 [T] (both walls, after the parabolic distribution of SC90C, Sjögren p. 23); tip clearance only above 80 % span, uniform weight 5 (M1 step 4b; extent [KS] Zanoni p. 16, shape [T]); both average 1 over the span.
- Rotor 67 with these losses on (analysis/rotor67_validation.yaml `losses_on_diagnostic`) [H]: design PR 1.516, -7.0 % (A-1 limit 2 %); the two tip tubes choke at the exit; hub PR near peak 1.744 -> 1.721 against 1.609 measured. The hub excess is a work/deviation issue, not a loss issue. The losses therefore stay OFF in the acceptance run and in the fan design until radial equilibrium is in (user decision, M1 step 3b).
- Rotor 67 DF per design streamline at the design point (rotor67_reference.yaml `computed_diffusion_factor`) [H from KS geometry; since M1 step 4c with the geometry-based shock expansion, no tube at exit choke]: 0.39 (hub, span 0.11) rising to 0.46 at span 0.34-0.44, 0.41-0.44 at span 0.64-0.82, 0.40 at span 0.92. Lieblein DF is less meaningful in the supersonic tip flow (M1rel > 1 above span about 0.45).
- Loss correlation sources (M1 step 4): Howell annulus/secondary drag in the form of Saravanamuttoo et al., Gas Turbine Theory, and Dixon & Hall (cascade lift, drag and loss relations); Lakshminarayana 1970 (J. Basic Eng. 92) for tip clearance. All [G]: not in the M1 source set (full citations in the `tools/meanline.py` docstring).
- Radial equilibrium (M1 step 4, `solve_rotor_re`): simple radial equilibrium dh0/dr = T ds/dr + Vm dVm/dr + (Vt/r) d(r Vt)/dr [G] at rotor inlet (uniform inlet: uniform Vm, split by area) and exit (tube exit areas and area-centre radii moved, mass per tube fixed, under-relaxation 0.1, re-solving work, deviation and losses each pass). Limitation: streamline curvature and slope neglected.
- A-1 with radial equilibrium (`radial_equilibrium_diagnostic` in rotor67_validation.yaml) [H]: RE without the new losses passes (PR +1.3 %); RE with the losses on removes the tip exit choke but fails (PR -4.5 %, eta 0.850; limit 2 %). Near peak, hub PR 1.744 -> 1.666 (measured 1.609); mid-span and tip PR now 0.13-0.22 below measured. Not tuned (user rule). Acceptance run stays without RE and without the new losses until this passes.
- Loss model review (M1 step 4b, PDF pages):
  - (i) Profile loss data basis: 2D cascade data. SC90C takes it from Lieblein's DCA cascade correlation with endwall loss added separately by Wright-Miller (Sjögren pp. 22-23); Abrisha combines cascade profile loss (Koch-Smith) with separate shock and endwall terms (p. 37). Our Lieblein/Aungier fit is cascade-based, so endwall and tip losses are not double counted; nothing removed.
  - (ii) Transonic shock loss: Schwenk normal shock at the passage Mach = mean of M1rel and the peak suction-surface Mach (SC90C, Sjögren p. 24); Miller-Lewis-Hartmann and Koch-Smith (SATURN, Visentin p. 32; Zanoni p. 20); Boyer's modified Miller-Bloch, oblique shock then a normal shock if still supersonic (Abrisha p. 40). `tools/meanline.py` already uses the passage-shock form (mean of M1rel and the Prandtl-Meyer suction-surface Mach), not a normal shock at M1rel; unchanged.
  - (iii) Tip clearance spanwise extent: measured Rotor 37 efficiency falls sharply above 80 % span, from tip clearance flow, shock and casing boundary layer (Zanoni p. 16); SC90C spreads the whole endwall loss (which includes clearance) parabolically (Sjögren p. 23); Abrisha notes Lakshminarayana's radial-distribution model without giving it (p. 21) and shows a parabolic total-loss profile with minimum about 0.027 near mid-span (p. 54). Applied: tip loss only above 80 % span.
  - Result (`rotor67_validation.yaml` `loss_review`) [H]: radial equilibrium + all losses, design PR -5.9 % and near-stall PR -9.1 %, eta 0.834 vs 0.901: A-1 fails. Mass-averaged deficit shares at the design point: profile 0.028, shock 0.068, endwall 0.030, tip 0.024 (1 - eta = 0.163; measured near-peak deficit 0.072). Calibration proposal: ADR-014 (draft, not applied).

## M1 rules
- R-AM.1 The first M1 meanline code implements steps 1-7 at design point, then off-design (step 8).
- R-AM.2 Loss set for version 1: Lieblein D_eq profile loss, Schwenk-type normal shock at passage Mach. Endwall (Howell) and tip clearance (Lakshminarayana) terms and radial equilibrium are implemented [G] but used in design only after A-1 passes with all of them on (it does not yet, M1 step 4). Alternatives are kept as options, not mixed silently.
- R-AM.3 Validation case: NASA Rotor 67 (`learning/rotor67_reference.yaml`). Pass criterion in `analyses.md` A-1.
- R-AM.4 Meanline results are compared with the M1 budget only through `core.budgets.check_budget` (see `budget.md`).

## Fan design (M1 step 3, `analysis/fan_design.py`)
- R-AM.5 Blade loading: DF limit 0.6 at the hub and 0.3 at the tip (Abrisha p. 38) [KS], linear in inlet span fraction [T]; each tube gets the lowest solidity that keeps DF at its local limit, within the search bounds 1.0-3.2 [T]. A tube at the upper solidity bound with DF above its limit is reported as a failed DF check.
- R-AM.6 Metal angles: kappa1 = beta1 - design incidence (3 deg [T]); kappa2 iterated until the tube pressure ratio equals its stream target (outer FPR, inner pr_fan_inner from `read_icd`). (M1 step 3, without radial equilibrium. Step 4d: per-tube targets have no physical equilibrium with the full loss model; the stream mass-averaged PR is met instead, see the step 4d section. For user decision.)
- R-AM.7 Splitter at the inlet radius whose inner area share equals W21/W2 (uniform inlet, `solve_rotor` splits by area); tubes of equal inlet area within each stream.
- R-AM.8 Until endwall and tip clearance losses are in the tool, design efficiencies are optimistic and are reported as such, not tuned. (M1 step 3; since step 4d the losses are on and R-AM.10 applies.)
- R-AM.9 (M1 step 4e, user requirement; replaces the step 4 text below) Blade loading per stream tube, split at the sonic span of the M1 fan (inlet relative Mach 1 with the uniform axial inlet velocity, `summary.sonic_span_fraction` in `results.yaml`) [H]:
  - subsonic region (M1rel < 1): Lieblein diffusion factor not above the NASA SP-36 limits for inlet stages, rotor hub D < 0.6 and rotor tip D < 0.4 (SP-36 PDF p. 286, printed p. 268) [K]; between them the limit is linear in inlet span fraction, 0.6 at f = 0 to 0.4 at f = 1 [T interpolation]. Supporting text: rotor-tip loss rises for D above about 0.35, hub and mean sections follow the cascade trend up to about 0.6 (PDF p. 257, printed p. 239) [K]. Parameters `blading.df_limit_hub`, `df_limit_tip`.
  - supersonic region (M1rel > 1, where Lieblein DF means less): solidity not below the Rotor 67 solidity at the same relative span (hub 3.11 to tip 1.29, linear in span, Strazisar p. 2) [KS]. DF is reported there with the SP-36 line as information only.
  - Loading difference: tip loading coefficient psi = dh0 / U_tip^2 (U_tip at the inlet tip radius; M1: dh0 = cp (T13 - Tt2) of the outer stream from the target FPR and the budget efficiency; Rotor 67: dh0 = cp 288.15 K (TR - 1) with the measured near-peak TR at design speed and the design tip speed) [H]: M1 0.310, Rotor 67 0.258 (`summary.loading_coefficient`). The M1 tip is more highly loaded, so the Rotor 67 solidity floor is a minimum, not a sufficient condition.
  - Every tube is reported pass/fail (`tubes[].R_AM9`); a failure is a result, not hidden.
- R-AM.9 (step 4 version, SUPERSEDED by the step 4e rule above; kept for the record) Blade loading per stream tube against Rotor 67 at the same relative span (linear interpolation in `rotor67_reference.yaml` `computed_diffusion_factor`, tubes at exit choke excluded) [H from KS]:
  - below about 45 % span (subsonic Rotor 67 inlet, M1rel < 1): DF not above the Rotor 67 DF;
  - above about 45 % span (supersonic, where Lieblein DF means less): DF not above the Rotor 67 DF and solidity not below the Rotor 67 solidity (hub 3.11 to tip 1.29, linear in span, Strazisar p. 2 [KS]).
  It replaces the Abrisha 0.6/0.3 band of R-AM.5 for the redesign. The 45 % boundary is the Rotor 67 sonic span at the design point (M1rel = 1 between span 0.44 and 0.54 in `computed_diffusion_factor`) [H].

## Fan redesign (M1 step 4d, `analysis/fan_design.py`; ADR-015 budget-efficiency branch)
- Model: `solve_rotor_re` with the FULL_MODEL loss forms (endwall + tip clearance on, `subsonic_shock_taper` False, multipliers 1). Endwall data per tube: blade height = mean of inlet and exit annulus heights [T], chord = solidity x inlet pitch, running tip clearance 3.0 mm [T] (Rotor 67 1.0 mm at tip radius 25.7 cm [KS] scaled to the fan tip radius; CFM value doğrulanmadı). Shock expansion: tools default (linear camber) [T]; it over-predicts M_ss (Rotor 67: up to +0.23 at the tip). Option `rotor67_ss_turn` (Rotor 67 ss_turn at the same span) did not settle in the R-AM.9 pass (3-cycle), so it is not used.
- R-AM.6 as written (every tube at its stream PR) has no physical equilibrium with this loss model (trials [H]): the tip tube entropy is high, and simple radial equilibrium at prescribed work drives the tip Vm2 to zero (constant work: dVm^2 = -2 T ds); prescribed uniform PR runs away (tip tube 63 % of the exit area at eta 0.24, middle tubes choked). Applied instead [T, for user decision]:
  1. blade shapes from a per-tube design on the initial layout (constant work per stream at the level of the budget efficiency, `pr_distribution: constant_work`);
  2. fixed-blade `solve_rotor_re` and one kappa2 offset per stream (2x2 Newton, FD Jacobian, backtracking) so that each stream's mass-averaged PR equals its ICD target; the outer offset only below span 0.8 (tip tubes keep their shape) [T]; only physical equilibria accepted (converged, no tube at exit choke), because `solve_rotor_re` also has collapsed branches;
  3. R-AM.9 solidity pass on the equilibrium state with each tube's work held; repeat 2-3 until no solidity moves by more than 1e-3.
  Tube PRs then vary along the span; the stream averages meet FPR and pr_fan_inner.
- R-AM.9 not met (rule not met even at solidity 3.2): supersonic zone keeps the Rotor 67 solidity floor [T] (more solidity adds loss there without meeting the rule), subsonic zone takes 3.2; both reported per tube in `results.yaml`.
- Exit hub radius ratio 1.30 [T] (1.45 of step 3 gives the splitter-side inner tube more work than its equilibrium share even at 1 deg camber).
- Root stress: area taper = (t/c tip / t/c root of Rotor 67) x (chord tip / chord root)^2 [T analogue, G].
- Superseded in step 4e: per-tube blade values, the linear-camber shock expansion, the outer offset span limit 0.8 and the step 4 R-AM.9.

## Smooth blade (M1 step 4e, `analysis/fan_design.py`, `analysis/blade_profile.py`)
- R-AM.11 Section profile family: MCA type (multiple circular arc). Camber line of two circular arcs with a common tangent at chord fraction 0.5 [T]; the front arc takes the share `front` of the camber; camber-line angle linear along each arc [G circular arc, taken linear in chord fraction T]. Thickness: two parabolic arcs with zero slope at x_max [T], edge circles ignored. Suction surface = camber line + half the normal thickness: tan(beta_ss) = tan(kappa_c) + (t/c) tau'(x) / (2 cos(stagger) cos(kappa_c)), stagger = (kappa1 + kappa2)/2 (Sjögren eq. 2.9) [G thin blade]. front = 0.5 with x_max = 0.5 is the double-circular-arc (DCA) section, defined in NASA SP-36 as circular-arc upper and lower surfaces, a high-speed profile (PDF p. 204, printed p. 186) [K]; SC90C's profile loss, stagger and suction-surface Mach correlations are for DCA sections (Sjögren pp. 23-24) [KS]; Visentin's R4 fan uses MCA profiles (Table 2.2, PDF p. 36) [KS].
  - Rotor 67 family: TP-2879 does not name it; it refers to its refs. 11 and 12 for the aerodynamic design (PDF p. 6, printed p. 2). "MCA" for Rotor 67 is doğrulanmadı in the source set. Its Table I sections [H from KS] have a front camber share near 0.5 inboard (DCA-like) falling to 0.12 at the casing (flat front suction surface of a supersonic section), which the two-arc family represents.
  - Fan parameters: front share (quadratic), t/c (cubic) and x_max (linear) in span, least-squares fits of the 14 Rotor 67 sections [H fit of KS, used as T analogue for the M1 fan].
  - Shock expansion: Tube.ss_turn = kappa1 - suction-surface angle at the covered-passage entrance (same definition as `rotor67.suction_surface_angle`).
  - Check on Rotor 67 (`results.yaml` `profile_check_rotor67`) [H]: builder ss_turn minus the KS geometry ss_turn, with the smooth fits: +1.13 deg at span 0.92, +0.21 at 0.82, -0.08 at 0.73, -0.36 at 0.64, -0.59 at 0.54 (supersonic streamlines, where the shock loss acts); -1.4 to -3.0 deg at span 0.11-0.44 (subsonic, no shock loss in FULL_MODEL). A plain DCA gives +4.3 deg at span 0.92. Values from the run of 2026-10-26; the yaml is authoritative.
- R-AM.12 Smooth blade, work distribution and stream balance:
  - One continuous blade from hub to tip (the splitter is downstream). Span variable f = inlet span fraction of the tube centre. kappa1(f) = atan(omega r1 / V1) - design incidence (uniform axial inlet) [H, incidence T]; chord(f) quadratic [T form]; kappa2(f) = cubic + k4 B(f) [T form]; t/c, front share, x_max as R-AM.11. B(f) = C2 quintic smoothstep x^3 (10 - 15 x + 6 x^2), x = (f - f_split - start) / width clipped to [0, 1], start -0.25, width 0.5 (symmetric about the splitter) [T].
  - Vortex: free vortex in each stream (dh0 constant, r Vt2 constant without inlet swirl) [G], blended across the splitter, dh0(f) = dh_inner (1 - B) + dh_outer B [T blend]; dh_inner, dh_outer solved so that each stream's mass-averaged Tt ratio equals its level 1 + (PR_target^((g-1)/g) - 1) / eta_stream [H].
  - Stream offsets (user-approved one offset per stream [T]): kappa2 += delta_inner (1 - B) + delta_outer B, same C2 blend, so the offsets cannot kink the blade at the splitter. 2x2 Newton with fixed-blade `solve_rotor_re`; only `RotorSolution.physical` solutions are accepted (tools.meanline physical_issues; no local guard).
  - Design-mode equilibrium: the blade shape is designed on a simple radial equilibrium with every tube at its vortex Tt target and the stream efficiency (no loss correlation, `fan_design.design_state`, same `equilibrium_areas` relation as the tool) [G]. Pass 0 uses the budget efficiencies, later passes the meanline stream efficiencies of the previous balanced solution; repeat until chord and kappa2 at the tubes change by less than 1e-4 m and 0.01 deg. Reason (trials, M1 step 4e [H]): shapes designed on the initial exit layout or reshaped on the fixed-blade equilibrium either collapse at the hub (hub tube takes about 70 % of the exit area, efficiency below 0) or run the tip camber to zero.
  - Chord: lowest mean chord (linear programme over the three coefficients) with every tube at or above its R-AM.9 need on the design state and section-area taper (t/c tip / t/c root) (c(1)/c(0))^2 not above `mechanical.area_taper_max` = 1.0 [T, G: section area must not grow toward the tip]. Without the taper bound the root chord is a few cm. (Step 4e; replaced in step 4f by R-AM.13.)
  - kappa2 blend (step 4f, user requirement): the total kappa2(f) (shape + blended offsets) must not fall anywhere in the splitter blend region f_split - 0.25 .. f_split + 0.25 (no dip, no local extremum). Limit: s dkappa2/df >= 0 with s = +1 (`blading.kappa2_blend_direction` rising [T]: kappa2 rises from hub to tip over the blade), imposed in the kappa2 least-squares fit at 41 points (inequality-constrained least squares, active set on the KKT system; the constraint uses the offsets of the last balance) and checked on 401 points with a reverse-step tolerance of 0.001 deg (`results.yaml` `kappa2_blend_check`, test `test_kappa2_has_no_dip_in_splitter_blend`). A direction taken from each pass's own fit flipped between passes and the PR balance failed (trial [H]).
  - kappa2 blend curvature limit (step 4f): |d2 kappa2/df2| of the final blade over the blend region <= the largest spanwise curvature of the Rotor 67 exit metal angle, 340 deg per unit span fraction squared (Table I te_metal_angle, `blade_profile.rotor67_kappa2_curvature`) [H from KS]; `blading.kappa2_blend_curvature_limit` = rotor67_max [T choice].
