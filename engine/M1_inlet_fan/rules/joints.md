# M1 Joints

## J-1 Blade to disk: dovetail (M1.1.1 -> M1.1.2)
- Axial dovetail: the blade root (flattened teardrop section) sits in a disk slot (Fox 2023, p. 1439) [KS].
- Load path: centrifugal pull through the two contact faces; friction and contact pressure on these faces drive FM-1.
- Rule R-J1.1: contact faces are analysed with friction (coefficient range [T] until a source is found), with and without lubricant.

## J-2 Dovetail shim (M1.1.4)
- Alloy 718 shim between blade and disk contact faces; MoS2 dry-film lubricant on its disk-side faces (Fox 2023, p. 1439) [KS].
- Added to the CFM56-7B design for disks made after 2004 (p. 1440) [KS].
- Rule R-J2.1: the shim is a wear part; it is replaced, not reworked [G].

## J-3 Dovetail coating stack (on M1.1.1 contact faces)
Order, from the base metal (Fox 2023, p. 1439) [KS]:
1. Shot peening (whole root and airfoil).
2. Grit blasting of the contact faces.
3. Cu-Ni-In coating by plasma spray.
4. MoS2 dry-film lubricant; reapplied at each blade installation and relubrication.
- Rule R-J3.1: coating thickness and spray cooling are controlled parameters; overheating during spraying can relax the peening stress (p. 1450) [KS].
- Rule R-J3.2: relubrication interval is part of the design (record it in the maintenance notes of M1.1.1).

## J-4 Blade spacer and platform
- Elastomer-coated Ti-6Al-4V spacer per slot gives radial restraint; aluminium platforms with elastomer seals give circumferential restraint and the inner flowpath (Fox 2023, p. 1439) [KS].

## J-5 Spinner to disk, disk to LP shaft
- Spinner: bolted flange [T]. Disk to LP shaft: flange and bolts [T]; the shaft side belongs to M8, so the interface is agreed through an M0 CR.
