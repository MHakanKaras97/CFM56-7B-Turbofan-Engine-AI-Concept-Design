# M5 – High-pressure turbine (HPT)

**What does it do?** It takes energy from the hottest gas leaving the combustor and drives the high-pressure compressor.
It is a single-stage turbine that turns very fast and runs very hot.

**Why is it needed?** It supplies the large power the compressor needs. The engine's toughest operating conditions are here.

## Main parts
| Part | Function | Typical material | Manufacturing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nozzle guide vanes (NGV) | Accelerate the gas and direct it onto the rotor | Single-crystal nickel superalloy + coating | Investment casting |
| Rotor blades (80) | Extract work from the gas | Single-crystal nickel superalloy with internal cooling | Single-crystal casting, laser drilling |
| Disk | Holds the blades with fir-tree roots | Powder-metal nickel superalloy | Powder metallurgy + forging |
| Front and rear shafts | Transmit power to the compressor, sit on bearings | Nickel superalloy | Forging + machining |
| Front air seal | Directs the blade cooling air | Nickel superalloy | Forging + machining |
| Shroud | Surrounds the blade tips, sets the tip clearance | Cast nickel alloy | Casting |

## Why is it critical?
- **Gas hotter than the metal's melting point:** the blades survive only with internal cooling and thermal barrier coatings.
- **Creep:** under constant load at high temperature the blade slowly stretches; single-crystal material reduces it.
- **Life limit:** the disk and shafts are among the shortest-lived rotating parts of the engine.
