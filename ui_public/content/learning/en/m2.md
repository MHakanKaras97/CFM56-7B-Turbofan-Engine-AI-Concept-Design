# M2 – Booster (low-pressure compressor)

**What does it do?** Behind the fan, it compresses the core air in three stages. It sits on the same shaft as the fan
and turns at the same speed.

**Why is it needed?** The fan cannot compress the core air enough. The booster supplies denser air to the
high-pressure compressor and raises the overall pressure ratio and efficiency.

## Main parts
| Part | Function | Typical material | Manufacturing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rotor blades (3 rows) | Add work to the air, raise pressure | Titanium alloy | Forging + machining |
| Stator vanes (3 rows) | Straighten the flow, complete the pressure rise | Titanium / steel | Forging, machining |
| Drum | Holds the rotor blades, bolted to the fan disk | Titanium alloy | Forging + machining |
| Case | Outer flow wall, carries the stators | Titanium / aluminium | Ring forging + machining |
| Variable bleed valves (VBV) | Dump surplus air into the bypass at low power | Steel / titanium | Machining |

## Why is it critical?
- **Stall:** at low speed the booster and the HP compressor can mismatch; the bleed valves prevent this.
- **Ice and foreign objects:** as the first core part, it faces ice and sand erosion.
