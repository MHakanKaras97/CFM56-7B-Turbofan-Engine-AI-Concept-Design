# M9 – Secondary air, oil and gearbox

**What does it do?** With air taken from the compressor it cools the hot parts and feeds the seals. The oil system
lubricates bearings and gears. The gearbox drives accessories such as the fuel pump, the generator and the starter.

**Why is it needed?** Without cooling air the turbines would fail within minutes, not hours. A bearing cannot run
without oil. The aircraft's electrical and hydraulic power also comes from the engine accessories.

## Main parts
| Part | Function | Typical material | Manufacturing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inlet gearbox (IGB) | Takes power from the HP shaft | Aluminium/magnesium housing, steel gears | Casting, gear grinding |
| Radial drive shaft | Carries the power out through a fan frame strut | Gear steel | Forging + machining |
| Transfer gearbox (TGB) | Turns the drive direction | Aluminium housing | Casting |
| Accessory gearbox (AGB) | Drives the pumps, the generator and the starter | Aluminium/magnesium housing | Casting + machining |
| Oil tank | Stores the engine oil | Aluminium alloy | Welded sheet |
| Oil lines | Carry oil to the bearing sumps | Stainless steel tube | Drawn tube |
| Bleed duct | Carries compressor air to the turbine | Stainless steel | Drawn tube |

## Why is it critical?
- **Cooling air is not free:** every kilogram taken makes no thrust; too much raises fuel burn.
- **Fire and leaks:** oil and fuel lines are kept away from hot zones and designed with double safety.
- **Access:** accessories sit on the fan case for easy maintenance.
