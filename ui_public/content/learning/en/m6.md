# M6 – Low-pressure turbine (LPT)

**What does it do?** In four stages it takes most of the remaining energy from the HPT exit gas and drives the fan and
the booster.

**Why is it needed?** The fan is the most power-hungry part of the engine; the LPT supplies that power. Because the fan
turns slowly, the LPT turns slowly too, so it has a large diameter and many stages.

## Main parts
| Part | Function | Typical material | Manufacturing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transition duct | Carries the gas from the HPT to the larger LPT radius | Nickel alloy | Sheet / casting |
| Nozzle guide vanes (4 rows) | Direct the gas onto each rotor | Cast nickel superalloy | Investment casting |
| Rotor blades (162/150/150/134) | Extract work from the gas | Cast nickel superalloy | Investment casting |
| Disks (4) | Hold the blades | Nickel superalloy | Forging + machining |
| Conical support and LPT shaft | Transmit power to the LP shaft | Nickel superalloy | Forging + machining |
| Case | Outer flow wall | Nickel alloy | Ring forging |

## Why is it critical?
- **Weight:** many stages and many blades; the LPT is one of the heaviest modules.
- **Efficiency:** a small loss in LPT efficiency clearly increases fuel burn.
- **Vibration:** long slender blades need careful control of vibration and resonance.
